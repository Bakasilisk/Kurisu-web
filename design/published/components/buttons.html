One button, one treatment — a gradient pill on --brand-grad with white text and --shadow-brand beneath it, plus an inset 0 1px 0 white highlight along the top edge so the pill reads as lit rather than flat. Still no secondary or ghost variant. Hover brightens 6% and deepens the glow; active drops 1px and collapses the shadow to --shadow-1.

Badges
Active Banned Inactive

Tinted pills with a leading status dot. Both the fill and the border derive from currentColor — color-mix(in srgb, currentColor 12%, transparent) for the background and 30% for the border — so each variant still only sets a text colour and everything else follows. The tint is weak enough not to compete with the row-hover and "this is you" highlights these sit inside; the dot is what makes a status readable at a glance in a dense table.

They were previously outline-only (border: 1px solid currentColor, no fill). That read as unfinished next to the rest of the surfaces once those became lit, and gave a status badge no colour mass at small sizes.

The critical badge takes --critical-text, not --critical. The chart fill #d03b3b measures only 3.6:1 on the dark surface, so badge text steps to the lighter red that dark --series-6 already establishes. Same split on the good side: --good fills charts, --good-text sets type.

Focus
Focused (simulated)

A single global :focus-visible rule covers every interactive element — 2px in --brand, offset 3px, on a --radius-sm corner. Nothing opts out, and no element removes the outline without replacing it.

Links

Inline links inside tables and lists — member name — take --accent at 550 weight with no underline until hover. The rule is scoped to .links, .stat-table, .rank-list and .stat-list rather than bare a, but without it those links fall back to the browser default blue, which is close to unreadable on the dark surface.
