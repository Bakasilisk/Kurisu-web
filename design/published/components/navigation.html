Behaviour

Sticky at top: 0 on z-index: 10, over --surface-1 with a hairline bottom border. Where backdrop-filter is supported the background drops to 78% via color-mix and picks up a 14px blur at 1.6 saturation, so content reads through it as it scrolls under. The opaque surface is the fallback, declared first — nothing depends on the blur landing.

Structure

The wordmark takes margin-right: auto, which pushes everything after it to the right edge — so nav links, the current user and the logout link all sit in one right-hand cluster with no wrapper element. Links are --text-secondary at 500 and transition to --brand on hover.

Mark

The 9×18 pill left of the wordmark is a ::before on .brand filled with --brand-grad and lit by --shadow-brand. It gives the nav an anchor without needing an image asset, which keeps the app's no-external-requests policy intact — the only binary in static/vendor is the font and Chart.js.

Narrow

Under 640px the bar wraps rather than scrolls: flex-wrap turns on, the gap tightens to --space-3, and padding drops to match. The link cluster falls to a second line under the wordmark.
