Body

Body copy runs at .95rem on a 1.6 line-height, antialiased, with text-rendering: optimizeLegibility. Long-form prose is rare in this app — most text is a label, a number, or a table cell — so the base size is tuned for density rather than reading comfort.

.standing — the summary line under a page heading, at 500 weight in secondary text.

.caption — chart annotations like a growth net total or a heatmap peak, at .8rem in muted text.
