Spacing
--space-1.25rem
--space-2.5rem
--space-3.75rem
--space-41rem
--space-51.5rem
--space-62.5rem

Six steps, no half-values. --space-5 is the workhorse — panel padding, grid gaps, and the gap between a heading and what follows. --space-6 is reserved for separating top-level sections.

Radius
sm · 4px
md · 8px
lg · 14px
xl · 18px
pill · 999px

xl is the outer container radius — panels, chart boxes, the login hero. lg sits one level in: stat tiles and server cards, which are often nested inside an xl box and need to read as smaller than it. pill covers buttons, badges, progress bars and the small gradient marks. sm is for focus rings; md appears on the period selector and the switcher dropdown. Heatmap cells use a literal 5px, which is between sm and md and tuned to the 18px cell rather than to the scale.

Elevation
--shadow-1
--shadow-2
--shadow-3
--shadow-brand

Shadows are tinted toward the brand hue rather than neutral black. In dark mode they read as noise, so they tighten sharply and the border carries separation instead. --shadow-1 is the resting card state, --shadow-2 a mid step used by panels on hover and by the switcher dropdown, --shadow-3 the full hover lift on stat tiles and server cards, --shadow-brand the glow under buttons and the nav mark.

Lit surfaces
--surface-grad + --sheen
flat --surface-1

Every raised surface is top-lit rather than filled flat: --surface-grad runs from a lighter top edge down to the base tone, and --sheen adds a 1px inset highlight along that edge. The pair is what makes a card read as an object under a light source instead of a rectangle of paint — it does more for perceived quality than any single colour change. --surface-1 stays a solid for the places that need one: the topbar's blur base, the switcher dropdown, and the .chart-error overlay, which has to hide a canvas behind it.

Page column
main · max-width 1100px · centered · padding --space-5 (--space-4 / --space-3 under 640px)

Wide tables scroll inside a .table-scroll wrapper rather than widening the page; main's own overflow-x stays unset so one stray wide element can't drag the whole layout sideways.

Grid breakpoints

.panel-grid auto-fits at a 320px minimum. .charts takes a higher 420px floor, wrapped in min(100%, …): it always holds four boxes, and at 320px the 1100px column fits three across and strands the fourth alone on a second row — 420px caps it at two and gives a clean 2×2. .cards sits at 190px — the floor at which the widest real value ("10,210,553") still fits on one line inside the tile padding. .guild-list auto-fills at 220px. Below 640px the panel and chart grids collapse to a single column and the stat tiles drop to a 134px minimum.
