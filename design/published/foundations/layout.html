Six steps, no half-values. --space-5 is the workhorse — panel padding, grid gaps, and the gap between a heading and what follows. --space-6 is reserved for separating top-level sections.

Radius
sm · 4px
md · 8px
lg · 14px
pill · 999px

lg is the card radius — panels, stat tiles, server cards, the login hero. pill covers buttons, badges, progress bars and the small gradient marks. sm is for heatmap cells and focus rings; md appears only on the period selector.

Elevation
--shadow-1
--shadow-2
--shadow-brand

Shadows are tinted toward the brand hue rather than neutral black. In dark mode they read as noise, so they tighten sharply and the border carries separation instead. --shadow-1 is the resting card state, --shadow-2 the hover lift, --shadow-brand the glow under buttons and the nav mark.

Page column
main · max-width 1100px · centered · padding --space-5 (--space-4 / --space-3 under 640px)

Wide tables scroll inside a .table-scroll wrapper rather than widening the page; main's own overflow-x stays unset so one stray wide element can't drag the whole layout sideways.

Grid breakpoints

.panel-grid and .charts auto-fit at a 320px minimum; .cards at 190px — the floor at which the widest real value ("10,210,553") still fits on one line inside the tile padding. .guild-list auto-fills at 220px. Below 640px the panel and chart grids collapse to a single column and the stat tiles drop to a 134px minimum.
