The only form control in the app. A native <select> with font: inherit, sitting on --surface-1 inside a --radius-md border — the one place that radius is used. The border transitions to --brand on hover; focus is the global ring. The chevron stays the platform's own, so it matches the user's OS rather than needing a background image.
